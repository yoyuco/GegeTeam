Gege Team

Digital goods and services for online games, run on software we build with Claude.

What we do

Gege Team is a self-funded company in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, founded in September 2024. We sell digital goods and services for online games on marketplaces such as G2G, and directly through Discord and Facebook.

Our team of about five people handles purchasing, sales and order delivery. We run all of it on software we build ourselves.

What we have built

Operations app

Used by our staff at www.gegeteam.net (sign-in required). It covers orders for goods and services, inventory for both interchangeable and one-of-a-kind items, task assignment with progress tracking and proof-of-delivery uploads, role-based access, and revenue, cost and profit reports converted into one base currency.

Vue 3 · Supabase (PostgreSQL) · Vercel

Trading ERP

One of our product lines runs on ERPNext v15, with our own custom app and a dedicated web interface for our traders. It is in production, and we keep extending it module by module from written specifications.

ERPNext / Frappe · Python · Vue

How we use Claude

Building our software

Claude Code is our main engineering tool. We write the specifications; Claude Code writes most of the code and tests, and changes ship through pull requests.

As of October 2026, Claude Code co-wrote 605 of the 816 commits in our ERP codebase (started May 2026) and every commit to our operations app since January 2026.

Reading the market

We use Claude to analyze marketplace data from G2G, an online gaming marketplace.

Claude inside our tools Planned

Next, we are bringing the Claude API into the systems above:

  • Pricing. Read listings on G2G and other marketplaces, track price movements and suggest our selling prices.
  • Proof checks. Read the screenshots our staff upload as proof of delivery and match them against the order before it is closed.
  • Customer messages. Turn customer requests from Discord and Facebook into structured orders in our system.
